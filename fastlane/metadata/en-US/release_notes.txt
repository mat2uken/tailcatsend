Ponlet is now on the App Store.

- Connect to another device with a QR code, an invitation link, or a pasted invitation URL
- Send multiple files at once with live progress, and cancel any time
- Browse received files, open them, or copy their save location
- Two-way text chat with copy, share, save, and clear-history
- Send directly from the iOS share sheet without opening Ponlet
- Automatic connection route selection, with the route in use shown on screen
- Japanese and English display languages
- Telemetry setting (opt-out)
